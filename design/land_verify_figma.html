How it works & why it fits

  • WHERE Slots into the existing Add Farm → "Govt Survey Number" section — just adds an upload box below it.
  • FREE Uses your existing Gemini Vision (no new paid API). Owner downloads their own RoR/Pahani/7-12 free from the state portal & uploads it.
  • REAL The government document IS the proof — AI reads owner name, survey no, area, village and cross-checks against what was typed → "Document Verified" badge.
  • AUTO-FILL Extracted area & village flow into the form — less typing for the owner.
  • TRUST Verified farms show a 🛡️ badge to farmers browsing land — big confidence boost, no gated API needed. Works all-India (any state's document).
  • FUTURE Built behind a LandVerifier interface — drop in the APISetu Karnataka API (free, once approved) or a paid all-India API later, with zero UI changes.
