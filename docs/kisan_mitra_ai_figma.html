The rename

Kisan Mitra = "Farmer's Friend". Warm, vernacular, memorable — it feels like a buddy, not a bot. (Alternatives: FasalGuru, Kheti Saathi.)

Multilingual

Switch language anytime (Hindi · English · Kannada · Marathi · Telugu · Bengali). Each answer has a one-tap Translate and 🔊 read-aloud.

Smart follow-ups

After every answer, context-aware follow-up question chips keep the conversation flowing — your service already returns these; we surface them beautifully.

Voice + image

Speak a question (for low-literacy farmers) or send a photo → ties into Crop Doctor. Voice messages with waveform playback.

Quick-start + actions

Welcome screen with 6 tap-to-start topics. Answers carry actions: Buy the remedy, Scan leaf, Ask community — looping the whole app together.

Trust

"Expert-verified" badge, 👍/👎 feedback and regenerate — modern chat UX that builds confidence.
